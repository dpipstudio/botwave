## Welcome to BotWave 👋

Hello, radio enthusiast! You've just arrived at the home of **BotWave**, a distributed FM broadcasting system for Raspberry Pi.

BotWave can run on a single Pi by itself, or sync multiple Pis from a central server to broadcast audio together. It's powerful, but don't worry, you don't need to understand everything at once. Take your time, explore, and have fun!

### Where should I start?

**I have one Raspberry Pi**  
Start with [Base/Setup](https://github.com/dpipstudio/botwave/wiki/Setup), then follow the **Local Client** path. No server needed.  

**I have multiple Raspberry Pis**  
Start with [Base/Setup](https://github.com/dpipstudio/botwave/wiki/Setup), then follow the **Client-Server** path.  

**I just want to look around**  
Check out [Main/Basic Usage](https://github.com/dpipstudio/botwave/wiki/Basic-usage) to get a feel for how BotWave works.

### What You Can Do

- Broadcast FM audio from files (MP3, WAV, FLAC, and more)
- Sync and manage multiple devices from one place
- Go live and stream audio in real time
- Build automated playlists with the queue system
- Use RDS to show station name and info on compatible radios
- Control everything remotely over WebSocket

### Important Notes

- FM transmission may be regulated or illegal in your area. Always check local laws before broadcasting.
- Only Raspberry Pi models 2, 3, 4, and Zero are supported. Pi 5 and Pico are **not** compatible.
