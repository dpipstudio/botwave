BotWave

BotWave – Your Raspberry Pi FM network. ¹

What it is

BotWave lets you broadcast audio over FM radio using cheap Raspberry Pi devices. It supports both single-device setups and multi-Pi networks, with features like remote control, live streaming, automated actions, and more, making it great for learning, experimentation, and creative projects.
